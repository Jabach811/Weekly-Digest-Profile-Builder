Deck or Single-Page?

Same retro-console theme either way. The question is the shape of the artifact.

A

Multi-slide deck (like the weekly brief)

What it is: Paginated. Arrow keys, slide 1 of N. Each section gets its own canvas.

Feels like: a formal presentation. Can be presented.

Pros: reuses 100% of weekly brief CSS & components. Good for big surface (all-plans, OOO pack).

Cons: overkill for a single-plan or single-project dump. One slide deck feels weird. Chrome (nav, slide counter) wastes space on small dumps.

B

Single long-scroll page

What it is: one HTML doc, no pagination. Sections stack vertically. Scroll to read it all.

Feels like: a briefing doc. Read-through, share-as-link, print-to-PDF friendly.

Pros: one artifact. Works at any length. Much better for single-entity dumps (one plan, one project). Better for phone/email share.

Cons: reading-mode, not presenting-mode. No keyboard nav. Less theatrical for a big all-plans dump.

C

Mixed — pick the right shape per output

What it is: each output in the family uses whichever form fits.

My read: all-plans snapshot = deck (many plans, each deserves a slide). OOO hand-off = deck (3-5 slides: frame · plans in window · contacts · lab · close). Single-project dump = single page (one entity, read-through).

Pros: each artifact is the right shape for its job. Nobody scrolls past a 14-plan deck, nobody paginates a single-project doc.

Cons: two build paths instead of one. Slightly more design work.
